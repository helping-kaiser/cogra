cora

Lowercase always. The mark stands in for the g at its real advance (601) and left sidebearing (30), so the spacing matches rather than approximates it; the bowl's top sits on the x-height line with the font's own 12-unit overshoot, and the tail drops the glyph's 213 units below the baseline. Never set the wordmark in another family, and never capitalise it — the product name in prose is "CoGra".
