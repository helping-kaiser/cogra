The layer is currentColor, which is the right ink for every variant without a second token: onPrimary on a filled button, primary on an unfilled one. The focus ring is onSurface, not primary — a primary ring disappears against a filled primary button. Press is never a scale-down and never a shadow change. Any pressable element gets all of this from class="cg-state cg-focus".
