# RankPath · `spec:design:behavior-rank-path`

ALWAYS the path's facts stand before its steps

ALWAYS the path states what it adds, and no step carries a number of its own

ALWAYS the path's newest age is its last step's

ALWAYS every age on the path reads on the ages ladder

ALWAYS a publish by the post's author counts as a step, its age the post's own

ALWAYS a step is one record, a record with two legs reading as one step that names the thing it passed through, and the trace above the steps shows the people and the reached thing's mark, never a thing passed through

ALWAYS a step names the thing the path reached as it, any other post by its title, a comment by its author's handle, a person by their handle and a topic by its #name

ALWAYS an opinion's step reads Your opinion of @ada, or @ada's opinion of @tobias when another person gave it

ALWAYS a publish's step reads @ada published it, and a comment reached through its own writing reads @ada wrote it

ALWAYS a comment's step that passes through the comment to what it answers reads @ada replied to it

ALWAYS a citation's step reads @ada cited it in, then the citing post's title, as @ada cited it in "The long way home"

ALWAYS a tag's step reads @ada tagged it, then the topic, as @ada tagged it #saltmaps

ALWAYS a step of feeling about a topic reads How you feel about #saltmaps, or How @ada feels about #saltmaps when another person holds it

ALWAYS a step the reader gave reads in the second person GIVEN the feed is the reader's own

ALWAYS a step the borrowed view's account gave reads in the third person, as @mira's opinion of @ada, never in the second person, GIVEN the feed borrows a view
