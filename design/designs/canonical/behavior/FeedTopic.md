# FeedTopic · `spec:design:behavior-feed-topic`

ALWAYS the filter trigger names the topic by the tag's own name, leading the extras after the kinds GIVEN a topic is on

ALWAYS the topic collapses with the rest of the extras into a count of changes GIVEN the extras do not fit the trigger's 154px

ALWAYS the trigger's kinds stay spelled out GIVEN the extras collapse to a count

ALWAYS the feed holds only content reaching the reader over Tag records toward the topic GIVEN a topic is on

ALWAYS the feed ranks a topic's content by the same primitive as every other feed GIVEN a topic is on

ALWAYS only a topic the reader holds an opinion for, with a netted Affinity that is not (0, 0) and associates positively, can be on

ALWAYS a topic the reader holds against never stands as the filter's topic

ALWAYS the feed draws nothing else differently GIVEN a topic is on
