# ReplyCompose · `spec:design:behavior-reply-compose`

ALWAYS the reply composer quotes what it answers above the words, a post by its title and its author's handle

ALWAYS the reply composer pre-fills nothing

ALWAYS the reply's words are a growing field at a three-line minimum, capped at 2,000 characters

ALWAYS the reply composer keeps no draft

WHEN tap + Add pictures or a video -> the platform's own picker opens AND NEVER a pick stage opens

WHEN picked pictures come back from the picker -> they land in the composer's tray

WHEN a picked video comes back from the picker -> the composer takes its video state

WHEN a pick is over its cap or in a format nothing here reads -> the composer says why
