# HistoryEmpty · `spec:design:behavior-history-empty`

ALWAYS History reads Nothing here yet. Everything you see shows up here on its own, newest first. GIVEN the reader has seen nothing yet

ALWAYS the empty History offers no action button and wears no error colour

ALWAYS the empty History draws no search field and no filter trigger

WHEN a content is fully in the viewport for the first time -> it joins History AND NEVER the reader has to act for it

WHEN tap the back arrow -> the reader's own profile comes back, in the state it was left

ALWAYS the back arrow reads Back to your profile
