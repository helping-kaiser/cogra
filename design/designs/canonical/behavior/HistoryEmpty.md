# HistoryEmpty · `spec:design:behavior-history-empty`

ALWAYS History reads Nothing here yet. Posts you read show up here on their own, newest first. GIVEN the reader has seen no post yet

ALWAYS the empty History offers no action button and wears no error colour

WHEN the reader sees a post -> the post joins History AND NEVER the reader has to act for it

WHEN tap the back arrow -> the reader's own profile comes back, in the state it was left

ALWAYS the back arrow reads Back to your profile
