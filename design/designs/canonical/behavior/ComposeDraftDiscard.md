# ComposeDraftDiscard · `spec:design:behavior-compose-draft-discard`

ALWAYS Keep the draft is the filled answer and Discard it the quiet one

ALWAYS the ask wears no error colour

WHEN press Discard it -> the draft is discarded AND the pictures come into reach AND a fresh pick begins

WHEN press Keep the draft -> the ask closes AND the draft stands AND its card still offers Continue

WHEN tap the scrim -> the ask closes AND the screen stands exactly as it was AND the draft is kept
