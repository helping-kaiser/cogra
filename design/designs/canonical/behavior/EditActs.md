# EditActs · `spec:design:behavior-edit-acts`

ALWAYS the edit beneath the sheet stays inert while the sheet is up

ALWAYS the sheet's title carries the batch's count

ALWAYS the acts card lists every act in the batch, a withdrawal counted as an act

ALWAYS the acts card carries the line They land together, or none does. GIVEN the batch holds more than one act

WHEN press Done -> the sheet closes to the edit

WHEN tap the scrim -> the sheet closes to the edit
