# PadPending · `spec:design:behavior-pad-pending`

ALWAYS the anchor wears the kept pick's face, never the bundle's resting face, GIVEN a pick is kept pending on the target and the key is not on this device

ALWAYS the quiet line Waiting for your key stands under the kept pick's face GIVEN the key is not on this device

ALWAYS a kept pick's line never reads Still settling

ALWAYS the bundle behind the target is what it was before the pick GIVEN the pick is kept pending

ALWAYS the anchor's accessible name says the opinion is waiting for your key, with the kept pick's word and pair

WHEN tap the kept pick's face GIVEN the key is not on this device -> the pad opens carrying the key notice, holding the kept pick

WHEN press and hold the kept pick's face GIVEN the key is not on this device -> the pad opens carrying the key notice, as a tap opens it AND NEVER anything is signed

WHEN the key is restored GIVEN the kept picks' review was left unsigned -> the line reads Waiting for your review AND the face's tap opens the kept picks' review

WHEN the key leaves this device again GIVEN kept picks wait unsigned -> the line reads Waiting for your key again AND the face's tap opens the key notice again
