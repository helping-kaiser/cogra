# ComposeWords · `spec:design:behavior-compose-words`

ALWAYS the body is words alone, never words beside pictures

ALWAYS Next stays in reach while the body grows

ALWAYS no count stands under the body GIVEN more than 500 of its 5,000 characters remain

WHEN typing leaves 500 or fewer of the body's 5,000 characters -> the remaining count stands at the end of the body's supporting row reading N left AND NEVER the count takes the error colour

WHEN typing passes the body's 5,000 characters -> the body takes the error outline AND the count reads N over in the error colour AND the line A post's words are at most 5,000 characters. stands under the body AND Next goes inert

ALWAYS the body's count counts the whole body in Unicode scalar values, never only what the box shows

WHEN press Next GIVEN the body is empty -> Next stays inert AND NEVER the details stage opens AND NEVER an empty post reaches a seal

WHEN press Next GIVEN a reference rides the draft -> the details stage opens with the reference staged

WHEN press Next -> NEVER the crop or the cover step opens

WHEN press the header back arrow -> the pick step comes back, one stage behind

WHEN press the header X -> the whole flow is left AND the draft is kept AND NEVER a dialog asks

WHEN tap Add pictures instead GIVEN the body holds words -> an ask to discard the body opens over the stage AND NEVER the body switches before the ask is answered

WHEN tap Add pictures instead GIVEN the body is empty -> the pick step opens AND NEVER an ask opens
