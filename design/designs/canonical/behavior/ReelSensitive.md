# ReelSensitive · `spec:design:behavior-reel-sensitive`

WHEN the clip on reel.stage is sensitive and unrevealed -> the stream stands full screen with the clip blurred under the standard veil, reel.stage.veil over it, AND NEVER the clip plays

ALWAYS a veiled clip has no playback and no sound-control presence

ALWAYS the stream and its veil stand below the system status bar, whose strip stays the system's, never painted black or any colour

ALWAYS the stream carries no reel.soundDisc and no reel.seekLine GIVEN the clip is veiled

ALWAYS reel.stage.veil reads Sensitive — tap to view over the source line naming whose mark it is, The author's warning or The platform's verdict, the reason after it where there is one

ALWAYS reel.caption.handle and reel.caption.title stay readable and reel.caption.description veils, reel.caption.show beside it

ALWAYS reel.rail's acts stay live over the veil

ALWAYS reel.rail.score wears the faces it wears on an unveiled clip — the figure, the bare glyph over Still settling, the dash — and stays the door to the post

WHEN tap reel.stage.veil GIVEN the device allows autoplay -> the clip and reel.caption unveil together AND the clip joins the stage's rotation AND it plays

WHEN tap reel.caption.show GIVEN the device allows autoplay -> the clip and reel.caption unveil together AND the clip joins the stage's rotation AND it plays

WHEN tap reel.stage.veil or reel.caption.show GIVEN the device suppresses autoplay -> the clip and reel.caption unveil together AND the clip stands paused on its cover with reel.playDisc, the card's own, in reel.soundDisc's corner AND NEVER it starts on its own

WHEN swipe up on reel.stage -> the next clip in the same list takes the screen AND this one stays veiled

WHEN the hold's signing does not go through -> reel.rail.stance.anchor.face stays where it was AND reel.rail.stanceLine beside it reads That didn't sign. in the failure voice followed by reel.rail.stanceLine.retry, Retry, on the stream's plate AND NEVER reel.snackbar carries the failure

WHEN the write rule refuses a hold's signing -> reel.rail.stance.anchor.face stays where it was AND reel.rail.stanceLine beside it reads You can't sign right now. in the quiet secondary ink, on the stream's plate AND NEVER reel.rail.stanceLine.retry appears

ALWAYS a reveal holds for every move inside the app until the app is fully closed or the media is hard reset

WHEN tap reel.rail.score -> the veiled clip squishes to the head of the post's detail AND the post rises beneath it still unrevealed AND NEVER a page slides in over the stream

WHEN tap reel.rail.stance.anchor -> the same pad opens over the veiled clip

WHEN tap reel.rail.comments -> the same comments sheet opens over the clip

WHEN press reel.backDisc GIVEN the stream was opened from the feed it narrowed -> the stream closes on the feed it narrowed

WHEN press reel.backDisc GIVEN the stream was opened from anywhere else -> the stream closes onto where it was opened, the state the reader left exactly

ALWAYS reel.backDisc reads Back to feed from the feed it narrowed, Back to the post from a post's clip detail, Back to the profile from another's posts, Back to your profile from the reader's own and Back to History from History
