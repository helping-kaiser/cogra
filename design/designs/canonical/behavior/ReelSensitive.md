# ReelSensitive · `spec:design:behavior-reel-sensitive`

WHEN the clip on the stream's stage is sensitive and unrevealed -> the stream stands full screen with the clip blurred under the standard veil AND NEVER the clip plays

ALWAYS a veiled clip has no playback and no sound-control presence

ALWAYS the stream carries no sound control and no seek line GIVEN the clip is veiled

ALWAYS the caption's handle and title stay readable and its description veils

ALWAYS the rail's acts stay live over the veil

WHEN tap the clip's veil face -> the clip and the caption unveil together AND the clip joins the stage's rotation AND it plays

WHEN tap Show on the caption's veil -> the clip and the caption unveil together AND the clip joins the stage's rotation AND it plays

WHEN swipe the clip -> the next clip in the same ranked feed takes the screen AND this one stays veiled

ALWAYS a reveal holds for every move inside the app until the app is fully closed or the media is hard reset

WHEN tap the score -> the veiled clip squishes to the top AND the post rises beneath it still unrevealed

WHEN tap the opinion face -> the same pad opens over the veiled clip

WHEN tap the comment count -> the same comments sheet opens over the clip

WHEN press the way back GIVEN the stream was opened from the feed it narrowed -> the stream closes on the feed it narrowed

WHEN press the way back GIVEN the stream was opened from anywhere else -> the stream closes onto where it was opened, the state the reader left exactly

ALWAYS the way back reads Back to feed from the feed it narrowed, Back to the post from a post's pinned clip, Back to the profile from another's posts, Back to your profile from the reader's own and Back to History from History
