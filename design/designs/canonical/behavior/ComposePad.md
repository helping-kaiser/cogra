# ComposePad · `spec:design:behavior-compose-pad`

ALWAYS the seal beneath the wash stays inert while the pad is up

ALWAYS the pad offers one axis, For or Against, and never a second

ALWAYS the pick is the post's own record, its backing, and with nothing standing it is also the result, so one readout suffices

ALWAYS the pick's readout stands above the field and reads Your pick over its face and one number

ALWAYS the readout's face is the one-axis table's face for the band the pick sits in

WHEN the pick sits exactly on the edge between two bands -> the readout shows the milder face, the one nearer zero

WHEN the pick sits at exactly 0.00 -> the readout shows 🤷

ALWAYS the pad opens at +0.10 GIVEN the author has not adjusted the backing

ALWAYS the pad opens at the pick the seal's Your backing row reads GIVEN the author adjusted the backing before

WHEN drag along the field -> the knob and the readout follow the pick

WHEN tap the field without dragging -> NEVER the pick moves

WHEN release a drag on the field -> NEVER the pick is staged

WHEN press Set GIVEN the pick reads other than 0.00 -> the pad closes AND the seal's Your backing row reads the pick AND NEVER anything is signed

WHEN press Set GIVEN the pick reads 0.00 -> the dialog Start it unreachable? opens over the pad, which stays parked beneath it AND NEVER the pick is refused

ALWAYS the dialog Start it unreachable? reads Your backing is your post's own path into anyone's feed. At 0.00 that path carries nothing — your post will live on your profile, reached only through the tags and citations you sign with it, until someone gives it an opinion. with Keep adjusting filled and Set it anyway quiet

WHEN tap Keep adjusting -> the dialog closes AND the pad stands at the pick

WHEN tap Set it anyway -> the dialog closes AND the pad closes AND the seal's Your backing row reads 0.00 AND NEVER anything is signed

WHEN press Cancel -> the pad closes AND nothing is staged

WHEN press the scrim outside the pad -> the pad closes AND nothing is staged

WHEN tap the pad's ? -> the pad's own help opens as a dialog over the seal AND NEVER the pad's body is replaced in place
