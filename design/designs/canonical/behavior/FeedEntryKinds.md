# FeedEntryKinds · `spec:design:behavior-feed-entry-kinds`

ALWAYS every ranked card's score opens the same four levels of the trace, whatever the card's kind

ALWAYS the trace's top block names a post by its title and author, a comment by its author's handle over its first words, a person by their name over their handle and a tag by its name beside its # tile

ALWAYS the top block's thing is carried down all four levels of the trace

ALWAYS the top block wears the score as a flag on the thing, its glyph and its signed number

ALWAYS a strongest path's trace ends on the thing it reached in that thing's own mark and speaks then what reached you for every kind

ALWAYS the trace's back arrow reads Back to feed GIVEN the score was tapped on a feed card
