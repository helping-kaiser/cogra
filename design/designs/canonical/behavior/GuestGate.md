# GuestGate · `spec:design:behavior-guest-gate`

WHEN a guest reaches for a stance, New post, the profile slot, the chats, Message, the comment sheet's foot or a comment's Reply -> the ask Join the conversation opens over the screen they were on with Posting and profiles need an account. AND NEVER the reader is bounced to another screen

WHEN a guest taps Save, Cite in a new post, Mention in a new post or Hide on a menu -> the menu closes AND the ask Join the conversation opens over the read

ALWAYS Share and License terms open for a guest without the ask

ALWAYS the read beneath the ask stays exactly where it was

ALWAYS only the ask and its scrim take a press while it is up

ALWAYS Sign in or join is the filled answer and Keep browsing the quiet text button, standing first

WHEN press Keep browsing -> the ask closes AND the read stands where it was

WHEN tap the scrim or press system Back or Escape -> the ask closes AND the read stands where it was

WHEN press Sign in or join GIVEN no invite link is held -> SignIn opens

WHEN press Sign in or join GIVEN a live invite link is held -> Join opens with the link in hand AND NEVER the invite door asks for the link again

WHEN press Sign in or join GIVEN the invite link held no longer works -> JoinInvalid opens

WHEN the guest signs in from the ask GIVEN the ask rose over an ask link's page -> the ask link's page comes back
