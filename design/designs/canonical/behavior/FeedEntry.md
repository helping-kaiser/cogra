# FeedEntry · `spec:design:behavior-feed-entry`

ALWAYS the trace lists the paths that reach the reader, strongest first, each stating what it adds

ALWAYS every path starts with an opinion the reader gave

ALWAYS the strongest handful stands drawn and the rest ride one quiet row stating how many there are and what they add

ALWAYS the drawn paths and the quiet row add up to the score the reader tapped

WHEN tap the quiet row of more paths -> the weakest paths unfold in place AND NEVER a second page opens

ALWAYS the trace carries no chart, no bar, no meter, no percentage and no trend arrow

ALWAYS the trace carries a cover of the thing its score was tapped on
