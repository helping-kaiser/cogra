# FeedEntry · `spec:design:behavior-feed-entry`

ALWAYS the trace lists the paths that reach the reader, strongest first, each stating what it adds

ALWAYS every path starts with an opinion the reader gave, and the note under the cover reads Every path here starts with an opinion you gave. GIVEN the feed is the reader's own

ALWAYS every path starts with an opinion the borrowed view's account gave, and the note under the cover reads Every path here starts with an opinion, then that account's handle, then gave., as Every path here starts with an opinion @mira gave. GIVEN the feed borrows a view

ALWAYS every path's trace leads with that account's face and is spoken from its handle, never from You, GIVEN the feed borrows a view

ALWAYS a path's trace shows the people along it and the reached thing's own mark at its end, never a thing the path passed through

ALWAYS every path stands drawn, strongest first, one continuous list that pages in place as the reader keeps going, never capped and never folded behind a row

ALWAYS the paths add up to the trace's score to the cent, the figure its own fetch found, a tiny path printing its own exact figure by the tiny rule

ALWAYS the trace carries no chart, no bar, no meter, no percentage and no trend arrow

ALWAYS the trace carries a cover of the thing its score was tapped on

ALWAYS the back arrow reads Back to the profile GIVEN the score was tapped on a card on another's profile, and Back to your profile on a card on the reader's own

ALWAYS every figure on the trace — the held thing's flag and each path's figure — prints two decimals, a positive taking +, a negative −

ALWAYS a figure on the trace prints its magnitude in two significant digits, as +0.0031 or −0.00042, and never past six decimals, GIVEN it is not zero and under 0.005 either way

ALWAYS a figure on the trace reads +0.000001 or −0.000001 GIVEN six decimals would print it as zero and it is not zero

ALWAYS a screen reader reads each figure on the trace as printed, never the computed one
