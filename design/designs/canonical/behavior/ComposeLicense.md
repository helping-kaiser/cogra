# ComposeLicense · `spec:design:behavior-compose-license`

ALWAYS the seal beneath the sheet stays inert while the sheet is up

ALWAYS one Credit reading of three stands chosen

ALWAYS one Public record of use reading of three stands chosen

WHEN tap a Credit reading -> that reading is chosen AND the Credit reading chosen before is not

WHEN tap a Public record of use reading -> that reading is chosen AND the Public record of use reading chosen before is not

WHEN press Done -> the sheet closes AND the seal's License row reads the choice

WHEN tap the scrim -> the sheet closes
