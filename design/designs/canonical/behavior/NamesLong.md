# NamesLong · `spec:design:behavior-names-long`

ALWAYS a person row never wraps, the display name ellipsizing first and the handle staying whole until it alone cannot fit

ALWAYS a page title, a row's title and its aside, a menu row, the profile's handle line and a caption never wrap

ALWAYS the handle or name standing in a one-line place is the part cut with an ellipsis, and the words around it keep their room first, as @maximiliana_feathe… commented on your post

ALWAYS a one-line place whose handle ends the line clips at its end, which cuts the handle and keeps the words

ALWAYS a heading, a card's title and body, a dialog's title and body, a snackbar, a notice and the borrowed-view band wrap at their spaces

ALWAYS a handle or name in wrapping text never stands on two lines: it moves whole to the next line when the rest of the current one cannot hold it

ALWAYS a handle or name longer than a full line of wrapping text is cut at its end with an ellipsis, its trailing punctuation kept whole beside it

ALWAYS wrapping text is never ellipsized as a whole
