# LadderMax · `spec:design:behavior-ladder-max`

ALWAYS a feed card's title clamps to one line

ALWAYS a feed card's description clamps to two lines

ALWAYS a feed card's tags and citations share one line, never wrapping to a second

ALWAYS a tall picture stands at its own 4:5 shape at the card's full width, never shrunk to fit the screen

ALWAYS a card carrying a tall picture runs past the fold, and the reader scrolls to reach its action row

ALWAYS nothing but its picture's shape grows a feed card
