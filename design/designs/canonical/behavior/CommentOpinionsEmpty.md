# CommentOpinionsEmpty · `spec:design:behavior-comment-opinions-empty`

ALWAYS the comment's opinions sheet opens stacked over the thread, the thread still open beneath its wash

ALWAYS the comment's opinions sheet is titled Opinions on this comment

ALWAYS the comment's opinions sheet reads No opinions yet — yours would be the first. GIVEN no opinion is held on the comment

ALWAYS the empty sheet never scolds and wears no error colour

ALWAYS the comment's opinions sheet orders and draws its rows as the post's opinions sheet does GIVEN opinions are held on the comment

WHEN tap the scrim, swipe the sheet down, press system Back or press Escape -> the sheet closes AND the thread stands open beneath it as it was
