# YourTopics · `spec:design:behavior-your-topics`

ALWAYS Your topics lists every topic the reader holds and nothing else

ALWAYS a topic is held GIVEN the reader's netted Affinity toward it is not (0, 0)

WHEN a walk-back nets a topic's Affinity to (0, 0) and lands -> the topic leaves Your topics

ALWAYS a topic held against stands in Your topics as any other yourTopics.heldTopic does, with no mark of its own

ALWAYS yourTopics.heldTopic carries yourTopics.heldTopic.mark, yourTopics.heldTopic.name and yourTopics.heldTopic.value, the Affinity pair the reader signed

ALWAYS the rows stand strongest first, by association, down past nothing into the topics held against

ALWAYS yourTopics.heldTopic offers no removal of its own

ALWAYS Your topics carries no bottom bar

ALWAYS the word follow appears nowhere on Your topics
