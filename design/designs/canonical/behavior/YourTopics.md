# YourTopics · `spec:design:behavior-your-topics`

ALWAYS Your topics lists every topic the reader holds and nothing else

ALWAYS a topic is held GIVEN the reader's netted Affinity toward it is not (0, 0)

WHEN a walk-back nets a topic's Affinity to (0, 0) and lands -> the topic leaves Your topics

ALWAYS a topic held against stands in Your topics like any other held topic, with no mark of its own

ALWAYS a row carries the topic's mark, its name and the Affinity pair the reader signed

ALWAYS a row offers no removal of its own

ALWAYS Your topics carries no bottom bar

ALWAYS the word follow appears nowhere on Your topics
