# FeedNarrowed · `spec:design:behavior-feed-narrowed`

ALWAYS the filter trigger reads Posts GIVEN the filter is at its default and the reader has set no default of their own in Settings

ALWAYS the filter trigger speaks only the filter's deviations from its default

ALWAYS the filter trigger spells the deviations out GIVEN they fit the trigger's 154px

ALWAYS the filter trigger collapses the deviations past its kinds to a count of changes GIVEN they do not fit the trigger's 154px

ALWAYS the filter trigger reads settled only among its deviations GIVEN the feed's filter has Still settling off

ALWAYS the feed admits only what the filter admits, in the order the filter sets

ALWAYS the filter trigger carries no glyph
