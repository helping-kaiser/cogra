# KeyElsewhere · `spec:design:behavior-key-elsewhere`

WHEN the app opens signed in on a device the key is not on GIVEN the account has a backup -> the feed opens with feed.keyCard at its head

ALWAYS feed.keyCard.title reads Your key isn't on this browser on the web and Your key isn't in this app in the app

ALWAYS feed.keyCard wears the account-notice register, feed.keyCard.restore filled in inverse

ALWAYS feed.keyCard stands until the key is restored here

ALWAYS the feed reads as it always does beneath feed.keyCard

WHEN press feed.keyCard.restore -> Restore opens

WHEN tap feed.card.actionRow.stance.anchor -> PadKeyAbsent opens AND NEVER anything signs

WHEN press and hold feed.card.actionRow.stance.anchor -> PadKeyAbsent opens, as a tap does AND NEVER the modest opinion signs on the spot

WHEN tap feed.bottomBar.composeSlot -> the post wizard opens AND its seal asks for the key before anything signs

ALWAYS every other control on the feed answers as it does on Feed

WHEN the key is restored -> feed.keyCard leaves the feed
