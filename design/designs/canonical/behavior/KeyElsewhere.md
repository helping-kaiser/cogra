# KeyElsewhere · `spec:design:behavior-key-elsewhere`

WHEN the app opens signed in on a device the key is not on GIVEN the account has a backup -> the feed opens with the key card at its head

ALWAYS the card's title reads Your key isn't on this browser on the web and Your key isn't in this app in the app

ALWAYS the card wears the account-notice register, its Restore the key filled in inverse

ALWAYS the card stands until the key is restored here

ALWAYS the feed reads as it always does beneath the card

WHEN press Restore the key -> Restore opens

WHEN tap a stance face -> PadKeyAbsent opens AND NEVER anything signs

WHEN press and hold a stance face -> PadKeyAbsent opens, as a tap does AND NEVER the modest opinion signs on the spot

WHEN tap New post -> the post wizard opens AND its seal asks for the key before anything signs

ALWAYS every other control on the feed answers as it does on Feed

WHEN the key is restored -> the card leaves the feed
