# RankHop · `spec:design:behavior-rank-hop`

ALWAYS the step states its folded value, how many records stand behind it and when the newest of them landed

ALWAYS the step's value is the folded net of every opinion behind it, the same fold the pad shows as the current opinion

ALWAYS the note over the step names it by its step title and its place, as Your opinion of @ada — the first of this path's two steps.

ALWAYS the fold's note reads in the reader's own word, as You have given @ada two opinions. They add up to one, and that one is what carries — the same adding up the pad shows you as your current opinion. GIVEN the step is the reader's own

ALWAYS the fold's note names who gave the opinions in the third person and leaves the pad out, as @mira has given @ada two opinions. They add up to one, and that one is what carries. GIVEN the step is another person's or the borrowed view's account's

ALWAYS the step carries the limit an opinion can reach and the fold's note closes with Added up, they go past the limit an opinion can reach, so the step carries the limit. GIVEN the opinions behind it add up past that limit
