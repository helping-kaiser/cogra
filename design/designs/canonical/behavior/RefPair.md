# RefPair · `spec:design:behavior-ref-pair`

ALWAYS the surface beneath the sheet stays inert while the sheet is up

ALWAYS the sheet is titled by the citation it edits

ALWAYS the pick's readout stands above the field

ALWAYS relevance runs across the field and support up it, each from −1 to +1

ALWAYS the pair stands at +0.10 / +0.10 GIVEN the author has not set it

ALWAYS the readout shows the nearest stance anchor's face beside the exact pair, never the anchor's word

ALWAYS the readout's spoken reading names both axes with their values

WHEN drag on the field -> the pair follows the drag AND NEVER anything signs

WHEN press Done -> the sheet closes AND the staged citation carries the pair it was given

WHEN tap the scrim -> the sheet closes AND the pair is what it was before the sheet opened
