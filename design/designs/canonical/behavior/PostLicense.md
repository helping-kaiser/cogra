# PostLicense · `spec:design:behavior-post-license`

ALWAYS the license sheet stands over the post it belongs to, the post still open beneath it

ALWAYS a post's terms read the same from the reader's menu and the author's own

WHEN the reader taps the scrim, swipes the sheet down, presses system Back or presses Escape -> the license sheet closes AND the post stands open as it was

ALWAYS the license terms are never a state of the card
