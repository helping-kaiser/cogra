# LadderStates · `spec:design:behavior-ladder-states`

ALWAYS a sensitive post's media, body and description veil as one, and its title and tags stay readable

ALWAYS the veil names its source, The author's warning or The platform's verdict, with the reason after it where there is one

ALWAYS the veil wears no error colour and no warning glyph

ALWAYS the veiled content keeps its exact space, so revealing it moves nothing on screen

WHEN tap Show on a card's veil -> the whole card unveils at once

WHEN the reader leaves an unveiled post and comes back to it -> the post stays unveiled

WHEN the app is fully closed and opened again -> every reveal is gone

ALWAYS a removed post's card keeps its skeleton: the author, the timestamp, the opinion a reader can still give, the score and the comment count

ALWAYS every authored field of a removed post goes at once, and the mark stands in their place

ALWAYS the author's mark reads Removed by its author over The words and pictures are gone. The post's place in the thread, and every response, remain. GIVEN the author removed the post

ALWAYS the platform's mark reads Removed under the platform's rules over A passed proposal removed it. The decision is public. GIVEN a passed proposal removed the post

ALWAYS the author's mark and the platform's mark never read alike

ALWAYS a removed post's card carries no License terms row in its menu

ALWAYS no removal is silent, and no removed post leaves a gap without a mark
