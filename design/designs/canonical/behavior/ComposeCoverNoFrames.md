# ComposeCoverNoFrames · `spec:design:behavior-compose-cover-no-frames`

WHEN the device returns no frames from the clip -> the frame strip goes AND only the way out to a picture of the author's own stands, under a line saying why

ALWAYS the preview is the neutral tile at the clip's own shape, with the duration and without the play disc

ALWAYS Next answers GIVEN nothing is chosen

WHEN press Next -> the details stage opens with a cover or without one AND NEVER the post waits on a frame the device could not cut

WHEN tap the picture tile -> the device's own picker opens AND on the web the browser's file dialog opens

WHEN a picture of the author's own is picked for the cover -> it passes the crop locked to the clip's shape AND only the cropped export leaves the device

WHEN press the header back arrow GIVEN a cover came from the gallery -> the pick step shows the clip wearing the chosen cover

WHEN press the header back arrow GIVEN no cover was chosen -> the pick step comes back with the clip staged

WHEN press the header X -> the whole flow is left AND the draft is kept AND NEVER a dialog asks
