# ComposeCoverNoFrames · `spec:design:behavior-compose-cover-no-frames`

WHEN the device returns no frames from the clip -> the frame strip goes AND only the way out to a picture of the author's own stands, under a line saying why

ALWAYS the preview is the neutral tile at the clip's own shape, with the duration and without the play disc, GIVEN no cover is chosen

WHEN a picture of the author's own comes back from the cover's crop -> it stands in the picture tile's place, outlined as chosen AND the preview shows it at the clip's own shape

WHEN tap the author's own picture in the strip -> the device's own picker opens again AND the picture it brings passes the cover's crop AND NEVER the picture is re-framed in place

ALWAYS Next answers GIVEN nothing is chosen

WHEN press Next -> the details stage opens with a cover or without one AND NEVER the post waits on a frame the device could not cut

WHEN tap the picture tile -> the device's own picker opens AND on the web the browser's file dialog opens

WHEN a picture of the author's own is picked for the cover -> it passes the crop locked to the clip's shape AND only the cropped export leaves the device

WHEN press the header back arrow GIVEN a cover came from the gallery -> the pick step shows the chosen cover beside the clip as its own tile

WHEN press the header back arrow GIVEN no cover was chosen -> the pick step comes back with the clip staged

WHEN press the header X -> the whole flow is left for where the composer was opened from AND the draft is kept AND NEVER a dialog asks
