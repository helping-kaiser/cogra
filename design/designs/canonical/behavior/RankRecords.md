# RankRecords · `spec:design:behavior-rank-records`

ALWAYS the records open under a line naming the step they stand behind, as The two records behind your opinion of @ada., in the third person for a step another gave, as The two records behind @mira's opinion of @ada.

ALWAYS the records' pairs add up to the pair the step carries GIVEN their sum stays within the limit an opinion can reach

ALWAYS the line Added up, these go past the limit an opinion can reach, so the step carries the limit. stands under the records GIVEN their sum passes that limit

ALWAYS the unclipped sum reads beside the step's pair in exact values GIVEN the records' sum passes the limit and the reader shows exact values

ALWAYS a record says what it is in its step's own title, the words RankPath gives the step for its family and its giver

ALWAYS a record row is inert

ALWAYS a record states its identity key in mono, for display only, with no control that copies it

ALWAYS a record older than thirty days wears a date in place of a rung of the ages ladder

ALWAYS the records close on the line These records are public. Anyone can run the same sum and land on the same number.
