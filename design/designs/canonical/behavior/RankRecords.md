# RankRecords · `spec:design:behavior-rank-records`

ALWAYS the records' pairs add up to the pair the step carries

ALWAYS a record row is inert

ALWAYS a record states its identity key in mono, for display only, with no control that copies it

ALWAYS a record older than thirty days wears a date in place of a rung of the ages ladder

ALWAYS the records close on the line These records are public. Anyone can run the same sum and land on the same number.
