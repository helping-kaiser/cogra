# ReplyMedia · `spec:design:behavior-reply-media`

ALWAYS a comment's several pictures share the comment's fixed square pager

WHEN swipe a comment's pager -> it pages between the comment's pictures

ALWAYS a comment's clip plays muted while it qualifies on screen, by the thread's stage law, in the comment pager's square frame

ALWAYS a comment's clip wears the sound disc and nothing else, with no play, no pause and no duration

WHEN tap a comment clip's sound disc -> sound turns on or off for every clip on every surface together

ALWAYS the reader's own comment wears Edit

ALWAYS a comment wears the Edited marker GIVEN it has been edited

WHEN tap the scrim, swipe the comments sheet down, press system Back or press Escape -> the comments sheet closes AND what it was opened over stands as it was
