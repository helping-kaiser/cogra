# InvitesEmpty · `spec:design:behavior-invites-empty`

ALWAYS Invites reads No invites out yet. A link lets someone make an account. Your vouch — the opinion you sign when you approve them — is what brings them in. GIVEN no application waits and no link is live

ALWAYS Create invite stands under the line, a filled button at the column's full width

ALWAYS the empty Invites carries no bottom bar and wears no error colour

WHEN tap Create invite -> the create sheet opens over the page

WHEN tap the back arrow -> the reader's own profile comes back, in the state it was left

ALWAYS the back arrow reads Back to your profile
