# FeedBare · `spec:design:behavior-feed-bare`

ALWAYS the feed reads from the genesis moderator's view GIVEN the reader is signed out and arrived with no invite link

ALWAYS the band names the borrowed view's account and carries Sign in or join

ALWAYS every stance face wears the borrowed view's own stance, read-only, at the member's anchor geometry, and the hollow face wherever the borrowed view holds none GIVEN the reader is signed out

ALWAYS the band carries no bell GIVEN the reader is signed out

ALWAYS the bar carries all five slots GIVEN the reader is signed out

ALWAYS the APK download line rides the band GIVEN the web during the dev phase

ALWAYS no APK download line stands GIVEN the app

WHEN an invite link that resolves to nothing opens the app -> the bare view arrives AND the snackbar reads This invite link has expired. once AND NEVER the band names an issuer

WHEN an applicant's account deletion lands the reader here signed out -> the snackbar reads Your account is deleted. once on arrival

WHEN tap Sign in or join -> the sign-in opens

WHEN tap the APK download line -> the browser downloads the APK

WHEN tap a stance face GIVEN the reader is signed out -> the guest gate rises AND NEVER the pad opens AND NEVER anything is staged

WHEN tap New post on the bar GIVEN the reader is signed out -> the guest gate rises AND NEVER the wizard opens

WHEN tap Profile on the bar GIVEN the reader is signed out -> the guest gate rises

WHEN tap the chats door GIVEN the reader is signed out -> the guest gate rises

WHEN tap Wallet on the bar GIVEN the reader is signed out -> the wallet's coming-soon page opens AND NEVER the guest gate rises

WHEN tap Feed on the bar GIVEN the feed is scrolled -> the feed goes back to its top

WHEN tap Feed on the bar GIVEN the feed stands at its top -> the feed refreshes

ALWAYS the newer-version snackbar announces on this feed as on the member's, once per release on a cold open
