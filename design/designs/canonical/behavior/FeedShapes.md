# FeedShapes · `spec:design:behavior-feed-shapes`

ALWAYS a 16:9 clip stands at its own shape in a feed card

ALWAYS a 1:1 clip stands at its own shape in a feed card

ALWAYS a clip taller than 4:5 stands at 4:5 in a feed card, centre-cropped and filled

ALWAYS a clip in a feed card fills its frame and is never letterboxed

ALWAYS a clip's stored still crops exactly as its clip does

ALWAYS nothing but the 4:5 clamp bounds a clip's height in a feed card
