# LadderSmall · `spec:design:behavior-ladder-small`

ALWAYS the author chip leads every feed card, above its title and its media alike

ALWAYS a post's body is words or media, never both

ALWAYS a card draws only the parts its post has, in one order: the author, the title, the body, the description, the markers, then the action row

ALWAYS a card's action row reads the opinion, then the score, then the comments, then the share

ALWAYS a wide or a square picture stands at its true shape at the card's full width, and the whole card, its action row included, sits inside the phone

ALWAYS a text body folds past eighteen lines, and More opens the rest in place

ALWAYS a card's face is the muted no-opinion face GIVEN the reader holds no opinion on the post
