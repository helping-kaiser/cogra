# SeveranceConfirm · `spec:design:behavior-severance-confirm`

WHEN tap Set GIVEN the pick nets the standing bundle to (0, 0) -> the dialog Walk it all back? opens with Your pick and its face and pair above the consequences AND NEVER the pick is refused

WHEN tap Walk it back on the pad -> the same dialog opens AND NEVER the dialog carries a pick line

ALWAYS the dialog reads, in order, its title, the pick line when a pick raised it, the consequences, the raw total of everything said, the cap only when the total exceeds it, the cost, and the fault line when one stands

ALWAYS the dialog names its target the way the pad names it, this post on a post's pad

ALWAYS the raw total leads and the cap line follows it, and the cap line stands only when the raw total exceeds the cap

ALWAYS the cost is counted in things: It signs 1 thing, paid on its own. or It signs N things, each paid separately.

ALWAYS Keep it is the filled answer in the right-hand slot and Walk it back the quiet one on the left

ALWAYS the dialog carries no error colour, the fault line aside

ALWAYS the dialog's words are the target family's own: a person or a post is walked back, a topic is disconnected from

ALWAYS the pad it was picked on stays parked behind the dialog's scrim

WHEN press Walk it back in the dialog -> the dialog stays up until the signing answers AND Walk it back refuses a second press AND NEVER Walk it back dims

WHEN the signing has not answered 200ms after Walk it back -> Walk it back reads Walking it back… AND NEVER a spinner appears

WHEN the signing is taken -> the dialog closes AND the bundle goes to nothing AND the snackbar says the signing is still settling and ends You've walked this post back to nothing.

WHEN the signing does not go through -> the dialog stays up with its pair AND the line That didn't send. Try again. stands above the pair AND Walk it back reads Retry AND nothing is walked back

WHEN press Keep it -> the dialog closes AND nothing is signed AND the pick is still parked on the pad beneath

WHEN tap the scrim, press the system's Back or press Escape -> the dialog closes AND nothing is signed
