# FeedProfileShapes · `spec:design:behavior-feed-profile-shapes`

ALWAYS a profile card's bio folds at two lines in the quiet colour

ALWAYS a profile card with no bio stands as the picture, the name, the handle and the row, with nothing in the bio's place

ALWAYS a profile card with no picture wears the monogram at 56px

ALWAYS a profile card's face is the reader's own opinion GIVEN the reader holds one

ALWAYS a deleted account's profile card keeps its disc's space filled with nothing, reads Deleted account in the name's place in the system's voice, and carries no handle and no bio

ALWAYS a negative score reads with a minus sign and no colour

WHEN a guest taps a profile card's face -> the guest gate opens
