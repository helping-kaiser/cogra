# CommentCitedByEmpty · `spec:design:behavior-comment-cited-by-empty`

ALWAYS the comment's Cited by sheet opens stacked over the thread, the thread still open beneath its wash

ALWAYS the comment's sheet is titled Cited by, as the post's is

ALWAYS the comment's Cited by sheet reads Nothing cites this yet — yours would be the first. GIVEN nothing cites the comment

ALWAYS the empty sheet never scolds and wears no error colour

ALWAYS the comment's Cited by sheet orders and draws its rows as the post's does GIVEN something cites the comment

WHEN tap the scrim -> the sheet closes AND the thread stands open beneath it as it was
