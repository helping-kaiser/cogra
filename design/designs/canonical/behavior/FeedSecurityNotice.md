# FeedSecurityNotice · `spec:design:behavior-feed-security-notice`

WHEN the first sign-in after a reused sign-in was caught lands on its shell -> the task card We signed out every device stands at the top of the column with A sign-in this account had already replaced was used again, which can mean someone else had a copy. If your password might be known to anyone, change it. AND Change password and Got it stand under it

ALWAYS the card rides whichever shell the sign-in lands on, an applicant's included

ALWAYS the card wears the olive account-notice register, with Change password filled in the register's inverse and Got it outlined

ALWAYS the notice is delivered exactly once, and never on a refused sign-in

ALWAYS the card's words never name the token beneath a sign-in

WHEN press Change password -> the credential screen opens AND nothing about the notice depends on what the reader does there

WHEN press Got it -> the card goes AND NEVER the card returns

ALWAYS the card stands until Got it is pressed: after Change password, after leaving the feed and coming back, and across an app restart

ALWAYS the band, the posts and the bottom bar beneath the card answer as they do on the feed
