# SealDiscardConfirm · `spec:design:behavior-seal-discard-confirm`

WHEN press Discard the post under the notice This shouldn't have happened -> the dialog Discard this post? opens over the seal with The draft goes, with its pictures, tags and citations. Nothing was signed, so nothing else changes.

ALWAYS Keep the draft is the filled answer in the right-hand slot and Discard it the quiet one

ALWAYS the dialog carries no error colour

ALWAYS the seal beneath the scrim stays inert, the notice's three ways out included

WHEN press Discard it -> the draft is gone, with its pictures, tags and citations AND the flow is left for where composing began AND NEVER anything is signed

WHEN press Keep the draft -> the dialog closes AND the seal stands as it was, the notice and its three ways out still offered AND the draft is kept

WHEN tap the scrim -> the dialog closes AND the seal stands as it was AND NEVER the draft is discarded

WHEN press the system's Back or Escape -> the dialog closes AND the seal stands as it was AND NEVER the draft is discarded
