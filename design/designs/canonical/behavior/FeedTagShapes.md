# FeedTagShapes · `spec:design:behavior-feed-tag-shapes`

ALWAYS a tag card's why-line reads Reaches you through @ada GIVEN the tag reaches the reader through one person

ALWAYS a tag card's why-line reads Reaches you through @ada and @tobias GIVEN the tag reaches the reader through two people

ALWAYS a tag card's why-line names the first person and counts the rest, as in Reaches you through @ada and 3 others GIVEN the tag reaches the reader through more than two people

ALWAYS a tag card's why-line compresses to Through with the strongest path's person GIVEN the full line does not fit its one line

ALWAYS a handle that does not fit even the compressed why-line ellipsizes

ALWAYS a tag card's glimpse line reads the newest thing's author alone GIVEN one thing carries the tag

ALWAYS a tag card's glimpse line reads the newest thing's author, then how many more, as in @tobias · and 2 more GIVEN more than one thing carries the tag

ALWAYS a tag card's glimpse leads with the newest thing's cover GIVEN the newest is a picture post

ALWAYS a tag card's face is the reader's own Affinity GIVEN the reader holds the topic

ALWAYS a tag card keeps its why-line, reads Nothing carries this tag right now. in the glimpse's place, and shows no age GIVEN nothing carries the tag

ALWAYS a negative score reads with a minus sign and no colour

WHEN a guest taps a tag card's face or its compose glyph -> the guest gate opens
