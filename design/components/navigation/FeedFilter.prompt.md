Use `FeedFilter` on every feed view — member, applicant, and guest alike; filtering is a read control. The trigger sits on the right edge of the `CograBand` (`trailing`), and scrolls away and back with it.

```jsx
const [filter, setFilter] = React.useState(FEED_FILTER_DEFAULT);

<CograBand trailing={<FeedFilter value={filter} onChange={setFilter} />} />
```

- **The trigger reads back the view in words, deviations only** — "Posts" at rest, "Posts · newest · showing seen" when flipped. No glyph: there is no filter icon in the inlined set, §5 forbids drawing one, and an icon cannot tell you Newest is on.
- **The pill has a budget, and it is pixels.** The head names one kind or counts them ("Posts", "3 kinds"); the exceptions are spelled while the whole reading fits the band's 154px and collapse to a count when it stops — "Posts · 3 changes". It measures itself in the trigger's own type (`measureTriggerText`), so no label can outgrow the pill quietly. A pill that overflows has told the reader nothing, and "far from the default" is the fact that matters at that point.
- **Kinds combine, order does not.** `FEED_KINDS` is the one list of ranked kinds, shared with search — Posts, Comments, Profiles, Tags, the kinds CoGra v1.0.0 serves — and the word is "Profiles" everywhere. Ranked-versus-newest plus the seen toggle live in the shared `OrderSection`. Do not turn either into the other.
- **It stages, and `Done` commits** (the sheet law, readme §4, *Sheets*). Chips, order, the seen toggle and `Reset` change the sheet only; the feed behind it is visual only and does not move. `Done` commits: a change to Kinds or the topic re-queries the feed once, and every other change re-derives on the device from what the feed already holds, never re-querying; the scrim, a swipe down, system Back and Escape discard. Once the ranker ships every refetch runs the whole personalized ranking — five taps must never mean five rankings. `FilterFoot` is the Done row: a hairline, `Reset` in the corner, `Done` at the end — no read-back of the staged filter, because the sections above are that.
- **The default is the reader's** (pass C 10). Pass `readerDefault` — the app's `FEED_FILTER_DEFAULT` until the reader sets their own in Settings, theirs after. The trigger speaks deviations from it and the foot's `Reset` stages it. Only the settings sheet's `Reset` stages CoGra's own default.
- **The topic section closes the body.** It is the one section that grows, so it comes after the kinds, the forms, the order and what else is admitted, and a long list never pushes those below the fold.
- **An empty filter is legal.** With no kinds selected the feed shows its empty state naming what is off; the chip does not refuse the tap.
- **The sheet carries its own "?"** (the pads' precedent) opening "The filter" dialog — the text lives in `guidelines/copy-voice.md` and names the settings default.
- **"Also show" is not a warning.** Sensitive content arrives veiled, a removed record arrives as its skeleton. Neither takes `error` colouring here or anywhere.
- **`Still settling` is on by default.** Content not yet landed is woven in among the landed rows, wearing its pending marker and ranked by the reader's reach to its author through the one edge it was born with; switched off, the feed keeps to what has landed and the trigger says "settled only".
- **People is a pair of rows, and the sign is one line.** `Everyone` or `Your people` — the people the reader holds an opinion of, the same rank narrowed to them — as radio rows, never a segmented pill. Only while `Your people` is chosen, one inline row under it reads `For · Against · Either` (default `Either`), the chosen word in primary ink: never a pill, never a second line. The trigger says `your people`, and `your people, for` or `your people, against` off the default sign. `FeedPeopleRows` draws the section; a static board drawing it in both states passes its copies as `peopleRows`.
- **Search wears the same pill.** A surface that owns its own sheet uses `FilterTrigger` with its own reading — same idiom, same silence at the default.
- **Settings owns the other half.** A surface whose own control is the trigger takes `FeedFilterSheet` — the settings page's Reading row opens it directly, so the default and a feed's own view are set by one control rather than two drawings of one. `lead` is what a titled sheet says first, and a sheet that covers what opened it needs a heading; the "?" then rides the heading's row, which is `SheetTitle`'s own rule.
- `defaultOpen` renders the sheet open, for static boards.
- Do not add a kind whose surface does not exist yet. A kind list follows the staging rule and shows only served kinds, never a dead chip; a door belongs to a slot, never to a list (readme §13, the v1.0.0 scope cut).
